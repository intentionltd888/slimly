## 中文

### Slimly 是什麼
Slimly 是一個 Mac app：把 PDF、照片（HEIC、JPEG、PNG）和 Office 檔（docx、pptx、xlsx）壓到寄得出去，畫質幾乎看不出差別。它也附一個 PDF 編輯器和轉檔工具。全部在你的 Mac 上處理，不上傳。

### 你可以用 Slimly 做的
- 壓縮、編輯、轉檔你自己的文件，或你有權處理的文件。
- 在你有權修改的文件上簽名、填表、加註解、塗黑個資。
- 拿掉你知道的密碼：你是文件的擁有者，或已經取得擁有者同意。
- 在家、在學校、在工作上使用。

### 你不可以用 Slimly 做的
1. 修改、散布、販售你沒有權利的內容，例如別人的作品、別人的證件。
2. 拿掉你沒有權利拿掉的保護。Slimly 不破解、不猜密碼，也不繞過作者設的權限限制。
3. 偽造或變造文件、簽名、證件，或用 Slimly 做出來的檔案詐騙、冒充別人。
4. 任何違反你所在地法律的用途。

### 你的責任
- 你處理的每一份檔案，有沒有權利處理、處理完怎麼用，由你判斷，也由你負責。
- 送出去之前，請自己打開檢查：壓縮後的畫質、塗黑有沒有遮乾淨、翻譯和摘要對不對。
- 簽名和文件在法律上算不算數，依你所在地的規定和對方的要求，請自己確認。
- 重要的檔案請自己留好備份。

### 我們不做的事
- 不經手、不存放、不上傳你的檔案。Slimly 沒有伺服器，也沒有帳號。
- 不破解、不猜密碼，不繞過權限限制。

## English

### What Slimly is
Slimly is a Mac app that shrinks PDFs, photos (HEIC, JPEG, PNG) and Office files (docx, pptx, xlsx) until they can be sent, with almost no visible loss. It also includes a PDF editor and conversion tools. Everything happens on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded.

### You may use Slimly to
- Compress, edit and convert your own documents, or documents you have the right to work on.
- Sign, fill in, comment on and redact documents you have the right to change.
- Remove a password you know, as the owner of the document or with the owner's permission.
- Use it at home, at school or at work.

### You may not use Slimly to
1. Modify, distribute or sell content you do not have the rights to, such as someone else's work or someone else's ID.
2. Remove protection you have no right to remove. Slimly does not crack or guess passwords, and does not get around permission restrictions set by the author.
3. Forge or alter documents, signatures or IDs, or use files made with Slimly to defraud or impersonate anyone.
4. Do anything that breaks the law where you live.

### Your responsibility
- For every file you process, you decide whether you have the right to process it and how to use the result, and you are responsible for that.
- Open and check the result before you send it: image quality after compression, whether redactions cover everything, whether translations and summaries are right.
- Whether a signature or a document is legally valid depends on the law where you are and on what the other party requires. Check it yourself.
- Keep your own backups of important files.

### What we don't do
- We never handle, store or upload your files. Slimly has no server and no account.
- We don't crack or guess passwords, and we don't get around permission restrictions.
- No fees, no ads, no donations.

- Slimly is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind, express or implied, including merchantability, fitness for a particular purpose and non-infringement. The license (`LICENSE`) governs.
- Compression re-encodes images. Slimly compares every page with the original and won't save a result that doesn't match, but it does not guarantee that every file looks the same.
- Redaction deletes the text and images inside the boxes. The same content outside the boxes, in attachments or in document metadata is not handled automatically.
- A signature in Slimly is an image placed on the page, not a certificate-based digital signature. Compressing or editing a digitally signed PDF invalidates its existing signatures.
- Translations and summaries are produced by macOS models on your Mac and can be wrong. Check important details against the original.
